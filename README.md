# SB 237 Child Welfare System Analysis

Technical policy dashboard for Louisiana Senate Bill 237 of the 2026 Regular Session.

## Overview

This repository contains a public facing digital analysis of SB 237, a child welfare bill that restructures reporting, investigation, school employee allegation tracking, fatality and near fatality transparency, and State Child Ombudsman access.

The analysis is framed as **watching and leaning support with amendments**.

The goal is not to dismiss the bill’s child safety intent. The goal is to pressure test whether the bill has the staffing, fiscal support, privacy safeguards, and implementation discipline needed to actually protect children without overwhelming the system.

## Key Issues Analyzed

- Expanded child abuse and neglect reporting pathways
- Immediate investigation requirements
- DCFS workforce and caseload capacity
- State Child Ombudsman data access
- LDOE school employee allegation database
- Fatality and near fatality public reporting
- Critical incident review process
- Fiscal exposure and implementation risk
- Equity implications for low income families, Black families, and families already overexposed to child welfare surveillance

## Policy Position

**Watching. Leaning support with amendments.**

SB 237 may be supportable if the final version includes stronger guardrails around:

- Workforce readiness
- Data privacy and role based access
- Due process for school employees
- Child Advocacy Center capacity
- Poverty and neglect distinctions
- Family preservation supports
- Transparent but trauma informed public reporting

## Core Frame

Child protection requires capacity, not just mandates.
